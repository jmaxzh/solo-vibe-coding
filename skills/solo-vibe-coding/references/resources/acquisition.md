# Acquisition references

Find existing marketing copy, emails, ads, and product-journey case studies. Select a source by the content needed; for writing or improving the product's own content, use [acquisition and conversion](../acquisition.md).

## Marketing Examples

<!-- resource: https://marketingexamples.com/ -->
- [Entry](https://marketingexamples.com/): marketing, copywriting, headlines, CTAs, landing pages, and distribution examples with editorial analysis.
- Select a concrete example for the current audience and message, retaining its original link and inspecting embedded images when they carry the content. Public articles provide references; reported results do not establish expected results for another product.

## Really Good Emails

<!-- resource: https://reallygoodemails.com/ -->
- [Entry](https://reallygoodemails.com/), including [welcome emails](https://reallygoodemails.com/categories/welcome): complete emails for onboarding, product launches, promotions, and other lifecycle messages.
- Reference a specific email's subject, copy, information order, or layout. Public previews, collections, workspace features, and source retrieval have different access conditions; obtain HTML only when the selected item and account actually provide it.

## Growth.Design

<!-- resource: https://growth.design/case-studies -->
- [Case studies](https://growth.design/case-studies): illustrated walkthroughs of real products' onboarding, purchase, retention, and conversion journeys.
- Use the captured steps and commentary to study flow friction and messaging. Viewing some cases requires registration; the authors' interpretations and proposed improvements are distinct from product-internal experiment results.

## Google Ads Transparency Center

<!-- resource: https://adstransparency.google.com/ -->
- [Entry](https://adstransparency.google.com/): published Google ads searchable by advertiser or website, with region and date filters.
- Use available browser access to locate actual ads and advertiser pages. Publication records support comparisons of copy and creative; conversion effects require separate evidence. Interactive search depends on the site's runtime and access conditions.

## TikTok Creative Center Top Ads

<!-- resource: https://ads.tiktok.com/business/creativecenter/inspiration/topads/pc/en -->
- [Top Ads](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/creativecenter/inspiration/topads/pc/en): existing advertising videos filterable by industry, region, objective, and other criteria, with creative and relative-performance information.
- Inspect a specific video's opening, script, pacing, and CTA. Logged-out browsing is limited; use the [official dashboard instructions](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/how-to-use-the-top-ads-dashboard) for current filters and access. Treat displayed metrics within their platform context.
