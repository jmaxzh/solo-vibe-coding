# Requirements references

Find existing product-document and user-story-map templates. Use previews for structure and retain the project's existing document conventions; copying editable platform templates requires the corresponding account and item access. For scope and acceptance decisions, use [requirements and communication](../requirements.md).

## Notion Product Templates

<!-- resource: https://www.notion.com/templates/category/product -->
- [Product templates](https://www.notion.com/templates/category/product), including [PRDs](https://www.notion.com/templates/category/product-requirements-doc): product requirements, roadmaps, feedback collection, and launch plans.
- Choose a specific template and inspect its preview, author, and free or paid access. Reuse the relevant structure in the existing spec or copy the template when requested; a template's fields do not establish the project's requirements or justify migrating its documentation.

## Miro User Story Map Templates

<!-- resource: https://miro.com/templates/user-story-map/ -->
- [Template catalog](https://miro.com/templates/user-story-map/): ready-made boards for task paths, user-story maps, prioritization, and release slicing, from Miro and community authors.
- Inspect a concrete board's layout and filled examples where present. Editing or copying depends on Miro access; adapt the useful structure to the current task rather than adopting an entire collaboration workflow.
