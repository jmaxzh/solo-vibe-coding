## Content distribution and building in public

For building in public, social handles stories, posts, platform adaptation, and content reuse; content-strategy handles themes, topic priorities, and planning. Use launch as needed for product launches, important features, and milestone announcements. Select content from actual prototypes, visual experiments, implementation challenges, or usage evidence; deliver drafting, scheduling, and actual publication as distinct outcomes. Adapt Chinese platforms to their specific context.

<!-- skill: coreyhaines31/marketingskills@social -->
- **social**: [Source](https://github.com/coreyhaines31/marketingskills/blob/main/skills/social/SKILL.md), [Skill page](https://skills.sh/coreyhaines31/marketingskills/social). Themes, stories, openings, platform adaptation, content reuse, and scheduling suggestions, covering X, LinkedIn, Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, and others; adapt Chinese platforms to their specific context.
- Its [ship-post](https://github.com/coreyhaines31/marketingskills/blob/main/skills/social/references/ship-post.md) reference identifies actual changes from commits and diffs, confirms users' previous difficulty and the resulting change with the developer, and then drafts distinct versions for X and the specified subreddit. Without user reports, do not invent pain points, feedback, growth, revenue, or claims of being several times faster. When no new feature is visible, describe that honestly or suggest not posting this time; do not portray dependency updates as fictional user outcomes.
- `ship-post.md` is a child reference of social; read it directly and remotely as needed. Distinguish drafting, scheduling, and actual publication, and deliver according to the user's request.
