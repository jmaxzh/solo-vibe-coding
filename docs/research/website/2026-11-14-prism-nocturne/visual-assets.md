## 最终提示

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: original cinematic hero artwork for an experimental premium digital-product website. Create an extraordinary large optical-glass sculpture suspended in a very dark graphite void. Subject: a complex, asymmetrical, non-closed knot made of three interwoven thick crystal-glass ribbons, with sharp folded architectural planes alternating with smooth liquid bends. It must have substantial physical volume, visible thickness, deep dark internal cavities, multiple front and rear planes and exquisite optical refraction, not a simple letter, not a ring, not a torus, not a flat zigzag. Material: smoked transparent glass, obsidian-clear edges, some luminous emerald and cool mint light traveling through the thick glass, with restrained violet and lavender chromatic dispersion on a few far edges. Stunning premium ray-traced realism, delicate micro-reflections, high contrast, extreme depth, elegant dramatic studio lighting. One coherent sculpture, not scattered debris. The sculpture fills the right two thirds of a landscape frame and slightly crosses the center, keep the left third mostly empty near-black for live HTML type; no bright light in the left third. Dark background approximately #070b0d with no floor, no horizon, no gradient panels. Tight sculptural framing, some scale and drama, entire important silhouette visible. Color hierarchy: mostly dark graphite and clear smoky glass, localized mint/emerald highlights and smaller violet spectral edges, no rainbow overload. 1536x1024 landscape. No text, letters, logos, icons, people, UI, screen, grid, label or watermark. This should feel like a refined optical art installation with cinematic depth, not a generic neon illustration or a sci-fi interface.
