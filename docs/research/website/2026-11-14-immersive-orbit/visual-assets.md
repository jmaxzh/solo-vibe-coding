Create an extraordinarily beautiful liquid-chrome torus / folded infinity ring, like a futuristic sculptural vortex made of polished silver metal and clear optical glass. Complex flowing smooth reflective folds, one large open central aperture, dynamic diagonal 3/4 perspective, dramatically realistic ray-traced studio lighting and fine mirror reflections. A thin cobalt-blue and electric cyan chromatic caustic along some edges, a small hot amber highlight along the opposite edge. The sculpture floats in a perfectly black void, with no horizon and no floor. Composition: widescreen landscape 1536 by 1024, sculpture centered slightly to the right occupying the right two-thirds, deliberately keep the left one-third very dark and nearly empty for live HTML typography to overlap. Premium futuristic art direction, sculptural, tactile, stunning volumetric reflections, luxurious contrast, crisp details, not cartoon, not generic neon tubes, not a flat diagram. No text, no typography, no logo, no people, no interface, no buttons, no watermark. Output fully opaque with deep black backdrop; object must stay fully inside frame with room around the outline.
